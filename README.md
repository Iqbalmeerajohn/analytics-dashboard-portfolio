## 🧠 Overview

A portfolio of **interactive business intelligence dashboards** built with Power BI, translating raw enterprise data into actionable KPIs and visual narratives. Each dashboard solves a real business problem — from predicting customer loss to analyzing workforce inclusion and measuring call center efficiency.

---

## 📁 Dashboards

### 1. 🔴 Customer Churn Analytics
> *"Which customers are about to leave, and why?"*

- Identified churn patterns across customer segments using demographic and behavioral data
- Built KPI cards for churn rate, retention rate, and at-risk customer count
- Visualized churn drivers using tree maps, bar charts, and slicers for filtering by contract type, tenure, and service tier
- **Business Value:** Enables proactive customer retention strategies, reducing revenue loss

**Key Metrics Tracked:**
- Overall Churn Rate
- Churn by Contract Type (Month-to-Month vs Annual)
- Churn by Payment Method
- Top Churn Risk Segments

---

### 2. 🟡 Diversity & Inclusion Analytics
> *"Are we building a truly inclusive workforce?"*

- Analyzed workforce diversity across gender, department, job level, and promotion rates
- Designed KPI scorecard for diversity health across hiring, promotions, and attrition
- Used clustered bar charts and demographic funnel to expose representation gaps
- **Business Value:** Supports HR leadership in setting measurable D&I targets and tracking progress

**Key Metrics Tracked:**
- Gender distribution by job level
- Promotion rates by gender
- New hire diversity ratios
- Attrition breakdown by gender and age group

---

### 3. 🟢 Call Center Performance Analytics
> *"Is our support team operating at full efficiency?"*

- Monitored agent-level and team-level performance with real-time KPI tracking
- Visualized call volume trends, resolution rates, and average handling time
- Built drill-through reports to investigate underperforming agents or peak-load hours
- **Business Value:** Helps operations managers optimize staffing and improve CSAT scores

**Key Metrics Tracked:**
- Total Calls Handled
- Average Handle Time (AHT)
- First Call Resolution (FCR) Rate
- Agent Satisfaction Score
- Call Volume by Hour / Day / Agent
